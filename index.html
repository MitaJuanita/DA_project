About Me

Hi, I’m Sherminta Lawrence — a Data & Interface Analyst with over 10 years of experience integrating clinical systems, optimizing HL7/FHIR interfaces, and transforming healthcare data into actionable insight.

I specialize in healthcare IT and analytics at the intersection of interoperability, reporting, and workflow optimization. From Epic to Mirth, from SQL to Tableau, I thrive in roles that connect clinical teams, IT, and leadership with data that drives better care and smarter decisions.

What I Do Best

  • Lead HL7 and FHIR interface migrations across systems like Epic, Meditech, and Cloverleaf
  • Validate and map healthcare data using SQL, Python, and REST APIs
  • Analyze utilization, performance, and claims trends to support population health and compliance goals
  • Bridge the gap between technical detail and strategic insight in cross-functional healthcare settings
