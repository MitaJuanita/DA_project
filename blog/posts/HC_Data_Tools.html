Healthcare Data Tools, Explained Simply

May 22, 2025

Healthcare Integration • 10 min read • Tools & Systems

I’ve spent the last few months bouncing between job posts, interviews, and rabbit holes of acronyms I didn’t recognize. Every listing mentioned a system I had to Google. Some tools I knew but hadn’t used in years. Others? Total mystery. Epic-this, Snowflake-that, something called Alteryx (still wrapping my head around that one).

I’ve worked in healthcare IT for over a decade. And still, I found myself asking: Am I underqualified? Or is the whole landscape just... opaque by design? Spoiler: It’s the second one.

Why I Made This Slide Deck

As I started to dig into the job market, I realized I wasn’t alone. Many of my colleagues were in the same boat, trying to make sense of a rapidly evolving landscape. So I decided to take a step back and look at the big picture. I wanted to get a bird’s-eye view of the platforms and systems we keep seeing — not just by name, but by function. Instead of sorting by vendor, I grouped them by what they actually do.
